The Story of Nouvelage®

From a single apartment in Nasr City to eleven branches across Egypt — a French-inspired journey of timeless beauty, told year by year.

Begin the journey
— Our Story

A vision, born in 2014.

Nouvelage® began with a simple belief — that beauty should age well. What started as one clinic, guided by the best of French expertise, has grown into one of Egypt's most trusted names in aesthetic medicine.

Scroll on to trace that journey — every branch, every collaboration, and every milestone that carried us from a single room to a nationwide house of care.

The Journey Map

Eleven years. One standard.

A chronological map of Nouvelage® — from Nasr City to the Mediterranean coast and beyond.

2014
Nasr City · Cairo

The Beginning

Nouvelage® is born — a French-inspired house of aesthetic medicine, founded on the best of French expertise. The first branch opens at Ramo Towers (Building 13, Omar Ibn El Khattab St.).

Founded by Dr. Randa, Dr. Pousy and the founding team — Asmaa & Mina Said — led by Youssef, founder of YouVentures, MWG & ZAT.

2015
Mohandessin · Giza

A Second Home

The Mohandessin branch opens, bringing considered care to the heart of Giza.

2016
Tagamoa · New Cairo

East Cairo Arrives

The Tagamoa branch opens — later relocated to a larger flagship at Cairo Festival City.

2017
Rushdy · Alexandria

On to the Coast

Nouvelage® expands to Alexandria with the Rushdy branch — the brand’s first Mediterranean home.

2018
Zayed · Giza

Growing West

The Zayed branch opens, serving Sheikh Zayed and greater Giza.

2019
Sheraton · Cairo

Closer to You

The Sheraton branch opens, deepening Nouvelage’s presence across Cairo.

2020
City Stars · Cairo

The Milestone Year

Nouvelage® opens inside City Stars Mall — the first clinic in Egypt to open within a major five-star mall.

A landmark collaboration with Hilton, Holiday Inn and City Stars.

2021
Alexandria · New Cairo

Two at Once

Camp Caesar in Alexandria and the Cairo Festival City flagship open in the same year.

2022
Mansoura · Delta

Into the Delta

The Mansoura branch opens, extending refined care beyond the capitals.

2023
Al Rehab · Loran

Twin Openings

New homes in Al Rehab (Cairo) and Loran (Alexandria) welcome their first guests.

2024
Madinaty · New Cairo

The East Hub

The Madinaty East Hub opens — a considered destination for New Cairo’s east.

2025
Mall of Arabia · Madinaty

Where We Are Today

Mall of Arabia (Sheikh Zayed) and the Madinaty East Strip open — bringing the network to eleven branches across Egypt.
